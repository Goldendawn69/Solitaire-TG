Rules

Build each foundation from Ace to King by suit.

Move tableau cards in descending rank with alternating colours. Kings may move to empty tableau columns.

Draw a 7-card hand from the stock. Play hand cards to the tableau or foundations when legal.

Refreshing the hand moves unplayed hand cards to waste, then draws a new hand.

Drag the top face-up tableau card to waste if you want to cycle it back later. This adds Curse Pressure.

When the stock is empty, the waste pile shuffles back into the stock and Curse Pressure causes transformation ticks.

Once all tableau cards are face-up, Complete can move available cards to the foundations automatically.
